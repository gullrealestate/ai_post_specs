# Gull Real Estate & Builders - Ramadan 2nd Ashra (Maghfirat) Post Specification

## Document Overview
This is a **standalone, single-event specification** for generating social media posts for **Ramadan 2nd Ashra — Maghfirat (Forgiveness)**, the second ten days of Ramadan. It is a companion to the main Gull Real Estate & Builders spec and follows all of its core identity rules (company name, contact, logo handling, prohibited elements). What this document adds is a **strict design language** and **10 distinct layouts** so the same event can be published with visual variety year over year.

All posts are event-oriented, deeper, penitent, and hopeful. The 2nd Ashra is the middle of Ramadan — a time of seeking forgiveness, deeper reflection, and quiet spiritual work. The visual register is **deeper, more reflective, and more grounded** than the 1st Ashra's soft dawn. The post should always feel like the eye has settled into a quieter, more contemplative space.

This is the second of FOUR Ramadan specs (1st, 2nd, 3rd Ashra + general Ramadan). Each Ashra has a UNIQUE color palette and must not cross-contaminate with the others. The palette and rules here apply ONLY to the 2nd Ashra (Maghfirat/Forgiveness).

## 2. Strict Design Language (Applies to All 10 Layouts)

This is the spine of the spec. The 10 layouts all live inside these rules.

### 2.1 Color Discipline
```
- ONLY THREE COLORS are allowed in the entire post:
  * Primary: #2E7D32 (rich emerald)
  * Secondary: #F5F5F5 (pearl white)
  * Accent: #E8B4B8 (rose gold)
- No fourth color. No exceptions.
- No dawn pink. No violet. Those palettes belong to other Ashras.
- Gradient stops are allowed ONLY between Primary and Secondary
  (the emerald and pearl white natural flow).
- The Accent (rose gold) is reserved for: subtle decorative accents,
  the company wordmark, and small micro-elements. Use rose gold
  sparingly — it is the warmest element, and the post should feel
  COOLER and more grounded than the 1st Ashra.
- Backgrounds: solid pearl white, solid emerald, or an
  emerald-to-pearl-white gradient. NOTHING else.
- Text contrast: AAA where possible, AA at minimum
```

### 2.2 Typography Discipline
```
- Use EXACTLY TWO typefaces per post:
  * One display face (the EVENT TITLE)
  * One body face (subtitle, message, CTA)
- The display face must be a refined, dignified serif (Didone or
  modern transitional) OR an elegant calligraphic Latin. NO
  playful or modern-sans display faces — this is the
  reflective Ashra.
- The body face is always a clean, highly legible sans-serif.
- Type sizes (as % of post height):
  * Event Title: 9-11%
  * Subtitle / Greeting: 4-5%
  * Wish Message: 3-4%
  * CTA: 3%
- Letter spacing on the title: +2% to +3% (slightly more expanded
  than 1st Ashra — the post is quieter)
- Title and message must remain readable at 200×200 thumbnail size
```

### 2.3 Composition Discipline
```
- One focal point only. The eye should land on it within 0.5 seconds.
- Grid: 12-column virtual grid; elements snap to thirds or halves.
- Negative space: minimum 18% of the post must be empty. More
  breathing room than the 1st Ashra — this Ashra is QUIETER.
- The CTA bar always sits in the bottom 12% of the post.
- The logo space always sits in the top-left 18% × 18% box.
- The event title is always centered on the dominant axis.
- REFLECTION RULE: the eye should settle. Avoid bright, busy,
  or high-energy compositions. The post should feel like the eye
  has come to rest in a deeper, more contemplative space.
- No element may overlap the logo space or the CTA bar.
```

### 2.4 Decorative Discipline
```
- Decorative elements are SYMBOLIC, never literal.
- Allowed motifs: small crescent, single small star, abstract
  cupped/curved lines (NOT literal hands), abstract dotted arc
  (counting days), stylized lantern (chiragh, no literal flame),
  geometric pattern at low opacity, arch/mihrab (line work),
  mist/fog bands, water/reflection motif.
- No more than TWO decorative motifs per post. Typically ONE hero
  motif and ONE supporting micro-element.
- Decorative opacity: hero motif 60-100%, micro-element 20-40%.
- Patterns are reserved for backgrounds only, at ≤15% opacity.
- No decorative element may be placed inside the logo space.
- Lanterns, when used, must NOT show a literal flame. Use a glow gradient.
- NO dawn pink, NO violet, NO ivory-dominant palettes. Those
  belong to other Ashras.
- NO literal hands, NO literal hearts, NO literal bodies in
  any position. The "Open Hands" motif is ABSTRACT CURVED LINES,
  not a hand drawing.
```

### 2.5 Mood Discipline
```
The 2nd Ashra of Ramadan is the middle ten days — Maghfirat (Forgiveness).

ALLOWED MOODS:
  - Reflective
  - Penitent
  - Deeper
  - Quiet
  - Sincere
  - Grounded
  - Hopeful (in a quieter, more grounded way than 1st Ashra)

NOT ALLOWED MOODS (would betray the spirit of this Ashra):
  - Celebratory (this is NOT Eid)
  - Festive with confetti/balloons
  - Solemn or mournful (this is the FORGIVENESS Ashra — it is
    hopeful, not heavy)
  - Bright or high-energy (this Ashra is DEEPER, not brighter)
  - Decorative-for-its-own-sake
  - Valentine's (no hearts, no romantic motifs)

If a layout choice starts to read as "Valentine's card", "Eid
card", "wedding invite", or "bright birthday card", stop. The
2nd Ashra is a deeper dawn — the eye has settled. It is the
reflective middle of a sacred month.
```

### 2.6 Aspect Ratio & Resolution
```
- All layouts default to 1:1 (1080 × 1080 px)
- Layouts marked [VERTICAL] use 4:5 (1080 × 1350 px)
- Layouts marked [STORY] use 9:16 (1080 × 1920 px) for IG/FB stories
- Resolution: minimum 1080px on the short edge
- Color mode: RGB
- Format: PNG (preferred) or JPG
```

## 9. Cultural Sensitivity Reminder (Ramadan 2nd Ashra-Specific)

The 2nd Ashra of Ramadan (Days 11-20) is dedicated to **Maghfirat (Forgiveness)** — the deeper, more reflective middle of the holy month. The visual treatment must respect that:

- **No celebration imagery.** This is NOT Eid. The register is closer to "deepened reflection" than "festival." The 1st Ashra was a soft dawn; this one is the eye settling into a quieter space.
- **No Valentine's imagery.** The Ashra is about FORGIVENESS, not romance. No literal hearts, no romantic motifs. The "Renewed Heart" mark (Layout 10) is a CRESCENT + ARC — explicitly not a heart shape, just a deeper version of 1st Ashra's mercy mark.
- **No dawn pink, no violet.** Those palettes belong to the 1st and 3rd Ashras. Cross-contamination is a documented failure mode of multi-Ashra posting.
- **No literal hands.** The "Open Hands" motif (Layout 02) is two ABSTRACT CURVED LINES like cupped parentheses. NO fingers, NO palm lines, NO wrist, NO skin tone, NO body.
- **No Eid-style festivity.** If a layout choice starts to read as "Eid card", "wedding invite", or "party poster", stop.
- **No religious text.** A greeting in English is fine. Verses and Arabic script are not used.
- **No worship scenes.** The Ashra is honored in the greeting, not depicted literally.
- **No mosque imagery with people.** A stylized arch or window is fine. A mosque with worshippers is not.
- **The dotted arc (Layout 04) is a counting line, NOT a tasbih / prayer bead / rosary.** It is abstract dots in an arc. No string, no thread, no beads on a cord.
- **This Ashra is DEEPER, not BRIGHTER.** Rose gold is used more sparingly than gold was in the 1st Ashra. The post should feel grounded and weighted, not warm and glowing.

The single most important rule for this spec:
> **Deeper, not brighter. The eye has settled. Forgiveness, not love.**

When in doubt: **subtract, don't add.** A simpler post is more respectful than a busier one.
