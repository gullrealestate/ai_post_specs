# Gull Real Estate & Builders - Ramadan 3rd Ashra (Nijaat) Post Specification

## Document Overview
This is a **standalone, single-event specification** for generating social media posts for **Ramadan 3rd Ashra — Nijaat (Salvation)**, the third and final ten days of Ramadan. It is a companion to the main Gull Real Estate & Builders spec and follows all of its core identity rules (company name, contact, logo handling, prohibited elements). What this document adds is a **strict design language** and **10 distinct layouts** so the same event can be published with visual variety year over year.

All posts are event-oriented, intensified, lifted, and anticipatory. The 3rd Ashra is the final stretch of Ramadan — the ten days that include **Laylatul Qadr (the Night of Power)**, the most spiritually charged night of the year. The visual register is **intensified, celestial, and upward** — deeper than the 1st Ashra's dawn, more lifted than the 2nd Ashra's grounded reflection. The post should always feel like the eye is searching the heavens.

This is the third of FOUR Ramadan specs (1st, 2nd, 3rd Ashra + general Ramadan). Each Ashra has a UNIQUE color palette and must not cross-contaminate with the others. The palette and rules here apply ONLY to the 3rd Ashra (Nijaat/Salvation).

## 2. Strict Design Language (Applies to All 10 Layouts)

This is the spine of the spec. The 10 layouts all live inside these rules.

### 2.1 Color Discipline
```
- ONLY THREE COLORS are allowed in the entire post:
  * Primary: #4A148C (deep violet)
  * Secondary: #D4AF37 (luminous gold)
  * Accent: #1A237E (midnight blue)
- No fourth color. No exceptions.
- No dawn pink. No emerald. Those palettes belong to other Ashras.
- Gradient stops are allowed ONLY between Primary and Accent
  (the violet and midnight blue natural flow), or between Primary
  and Secondary (violet and gold).
- The Secondary (luminous gold) is reserved for: type, decorative
  motif line work, and CTA icons. Gold is the LUMINOUS element —
  the visual signature of Laylatul Qadr's power.
- The Accent (midnight blue) is reserved for: deepest background
  shadows, micro-elements, and as a gradient partner with violet.
- Backgrounds: solid midnight blue, solid violet, or a violet-to-
  midnight-blue gradient. NOTHING else.
- Text contrast: AAA where possible, AA at minimum
```

### 2.2 Typography Discipline
```
- Use EXACTLY TWO typefaces per post:
  * One display face (the EVENT TITLE)
  * One body face (subtitle, message, CTA)
- The display face must be a refined, dignified serif (Didone or
  modern transitional) OR an elegant calligraphic Latin. The
  calligraphic option suits this Ashra particularly well — the
  flowing terminals echo the "ascending" mood.
- The body face is always a clean, highly legible sans-serif.
- Type sizes (as % of post height):
  * Event Title: 9-11%
  * Subtitle / Greeting: 4-5%
  * Wish Message: 3-4%
  * CTA: 3%
- Letter spacing on the title: +2% to +3% (slightly expanded)
- Title and message must remain readable at 200×200 thumbnail size
```

### 2.3 Composition Discipline
```
- One focal point only. The eye should land on it within 0.5 seconds.
- Grid: 12-column virtual grid; elements snap to thirds or halves.
- Negative space: minimum 15% of the post must be empty.
- The CTA bar always sits in the bottom 12% of the post.
- The logo space always sits in the top-left 18% × 18% box.
- The event title is always centered on the dominant axis.
- ASCENT RULE: the eye should travel from the type block UP toward
  the focal motif in the upper third. The composition should feel
  like searching the heavens for Laylatul Qadr.
- No element may overlap the logo space or the CTA bar.
```

### 2.4 Decorative Discipline
```
- Decorative elements are SYMBOLIC, never literal.
- Allowed motifs: small crescent (with POWERFUL gold glow), single
  small star, abstract dotted arc (counting days), stylized lantern
  (chiragh, no literal flame), geometric pattern at low opacity,
  arch/mihrab (line work), mist/fog bands, soft gold light beam,
  layered atmospheric sky bands.
- No more than TWO decorative motifs per post. Typically ONE hero
  motif and ONE supporting micro-element.
- Decorative opacity: hero motif 60-100%, micro-element 20-40%.
- Patterns are reserved for backgrounds only, at ≤15% opacity.
- No decorative element may be placed inside the logo space.
- Lanterns, when used, must NOT show a literal flame. Use a glow gradient.
- NO dawn pink, NO emerald, NO ivory-dominant palettes. Those
  belong to other Ashras.
- NO literal hands, NO literal hearts, NO literal bodies.
- PROCEED WITH CARE: any motif suggesting the heavens must remain
  abstract and atmospheric — not architectural, not structural,
  not a built thing meant to be climbed. The journey is THROUGH
  the heavens, not ACROSS a surface.
```

### 2.5 Mood Discipline
```
The 3rd Ashra of Ramadan is the final ten days — Nijaat (Salvation),
and the search for Laylatul Qadr (the Night of Power).

ALLOWED MOODS:
  - Intensified
  - Lifted
  - Anticipatory
  - Hopeful (in a powerful, searching way)
  - Celestial
  - Awestruck
  - Reverent

NOT ALLOWED MOODS (would betray the spirit of this Ashra):
  - Celebratory (this is NOT Eid — the search continues)
  - Festive with confetti/balloons
  - Solemn or mournful (this is ANTICIPATORY, not heavy)
  - Calm or restful (this Ashra is INTENSIFIED, not peaceful)
  - Decorative-for-its-own-sake
  - Valentine's (no hearts, no romantic motifs)

If a layout choice starts to read as "Valentine's card", "Eid
card", "wedding invite", or "calm Ramadan greeting", stop. The
3rd Ashra is a HEAVENLY SEARCH — the eye is looking up, the heart
is searching, the night is charged with spiritual power.
```

### 2.6 Aspect Ratio & Resolution
```
- All layouts default to 1:1 (1080 × 1080 px)
- Layouts marked [VERTICAL] use 4:5 (1080 × 1350 px)
- Layouts marked [STORY] use 9:16 (1080 × 1920 px) for IG/FB stories
- Resolution: minimum 1080px on the short edge
- Color mode: RGB
- Format: PNG (preferred) or JPG
```

## 9. Cultural Sensitivity Reminder (Ramadan 3rd Ashra-Specific)

The 3rd Ashra of Ramadan (Days 21-30) is dedicated to **Nijaat (Salvation)** — and it includes **Laylatul Qadr (the Night of Power)**, the most spiritually charged night of the year. The visual treatment must respect that:

- **No celebration imagery.** This is NOT Eid. The register is "intensified anticipation" — the search for the Night of Power. The 1st Ashra was a soft dawn; the 2nd was deeper; this is INTENSIFIED and LIFTED.
- **No Valentine's imagery.** The Ashra is about SALVATION, not romance. No literal hearts, no romantic motifs. The "Final Mercy Mark" (Layout 10) is a CRESCENT + ARC — explicitly not a heart shape, just the most weighted version of the three Ashra mercy marks.
- **No dawn pink, no emerald.** Those palettes belong to the 1st and 2nd Ashras. Cross-contamination is a documented failure mode of multi-Ashra posting.
- **No literal hands, no literal hearts, no literal bodies in any position.** This spec has no hands layout, but the rule applies to any adaptation.
- **No Eid-style festivity.** If a layout choice starts to read as "Eid card", "wedding invite", or "party poster", stop.
- **No religious text.** A greeting in English is fine. Verses and Arabic script are not used.
- **No worship scenes.** The Ashra is honored in the greeting, not depicted literally.
- **No mosque imagery with people.** A stylized arch or window is fine. A mosque with worshippers is not.
- **The dotted arc (Layout 04) is a counting line, NOT a tasbih / prayer bead / rosary.** It is abstract dots in an arc. No string, no thread, no beads on a cord.
- **PROCEED WITH CARE:** any motif suggesting the heavens must remain abstract and atmospheric. The journey is THROUGH the heavens, not ACROSS a surface. No literal stairs, no literal ladder, no pearly gates, no platforms, no floors, no tiers.
- **Gold is sacred, not gaudy.** Gold reads as divine light, not decoration. Use gradients and luminous quality, never flat yellow fills.
- **This Ashra is INTENSIFIED, not PEACEFUL.** The eye should travel UP. The search is active, the night is charged.

The single most important rule for this spec:
> **Intensified, not festive. Searching, not peaceful. The heavens are charged, the eye is looking up.**

When in doubt: **subtract, don't add.** A simpler post is more respectful than a busier one. If a layout choice starts to read as "video game UI", "Christmas card", or "calm Ramadan greeting", stop.
